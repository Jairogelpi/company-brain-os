# Pilot measurement scorecard

Capture a signed-off baseline before intervention and the same measures at final readout.

| Metric | Definition | Formula / source | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Critical exposures validated | Critical/high rule findings approved as real | Count of approved exposure records | Customer validator |
| Critical exposures mitigated | Validated exposure whose required control is independently approved and no longer triggers | Count after deterministic recalculation | Pilot lead |
| Critical knowledge coverage | Critical Knowledge with at least two qualified people or an accepted control | Covered critical / total critical | System + validator |
| Provenance coverage | Projected relationships with canonical assertion refs | Traceable relationships / all projected relationships | System |
| Verified transfer rate | Transfer missions with competency ≥3, access, evidence and independent approval | Verified / submitted transfers | System |
| Mission lead time | Time from assignment to verified closure | Median calendar days | System |
| Evidence rejection rate | Submissions rejected by validators | Rejected reviews / total reviews | System |
| Time to first exposure | Kickoff/capture start to first validated explainable exposure | Elapsed minutes/hours | Pilot lead |
