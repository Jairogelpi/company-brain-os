# Company Brain OS — continuity risk you can close, not just document

## The problem

When a critical employee, supplier or routine becomes unavailable, most companies discover too late that documentation did not create a capable backup. Conventional knowledge bases count files; they do not prove execution readiness.

## The outcome

Company Brain OS identifies concentrated operational knowledge, explains the exact facts behind each exposure and turns it into a controlled transfer mission. A dependency closes only after a backup demonstrates competency, required access and evidence under independent review.

## Who it is for

Owner-led and mid-market companies with 20–500 employees facing succession, retirement, acquisition, audit findings, rapid hiring or recurrent dependence on specialists.

## Eight-week pilot

- one operational area;
- 10–30 participants;
- up to 25 critical exposures;
- facilitated capture and weekly operating review;
- evidence-backed mitigations and executive readout.

Working price hypothesis: **€12,000 excluding VAT**, 50% at kickoff and 50% at the final readout. Travel, bespoke integrations and third-party AI/storage overage are excluded. Validate or revise this price through qualified buyer conversations and paid pilots; it is not presented as market evidence.

## Success

The primary measure is Critical Exposures Mitigated. An exposure counts only when its control evidence is independently approved and residual risk is recalculated. Supporting measures include time to first explainable exposure, provenance coverage, mission lead time and evidence rejection rate.

## Product boundary

The system measures operational dependency. It does not rank employee productivity, loyalty or performance, and AI cannot approve facts or employment decisions.
