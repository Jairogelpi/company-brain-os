# Commercial pilot offer

## Ideal customer profile

Companies with 20–500 employees, material operational know-how, and a concrete continuity trigger: retirement, succession, acquisition, audit finding, rapid hiring, critical supplier concentration or repeated dependence on a small number of specialists.

## Buyer and users

- Economic buyer: CEO, COO, operations director or owner.
- Champion: quality, people, transformation or continuity lead.
- Contributors: subject-matter experts and nominated backups.
- Validators: managers or domain owners with authority to approve evidence.

## 8-week paid pilot

Scope one operational area, 10–30 participants and up to 25 critical exposures.

| Week | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Security/data-processing review, tenant setup, success baseline |
| 2 | Continuity interviews and import; first explainable exposures |
| 3–4 | Human validation and prioritized mitigation missions |
| 5–6 | Artifacts, backup assessments and access checks |
| 7 | Independent approvals and residual-risk recalculation |
| 8 | Executive readout, evidence pack and scale decision |

## Success measures

Primary: Critical Exposures Mitigated. An exposure counts only after required evidence and control are approved and residual risk is recalculated.

Pilot scorecard:

- time to first explainable exposure;
- critical exposures validated;
- critical exposures mitigated;
- critical knowledge coverage before/after;
- median mission lead time;
- percentage of projected relationships with provenance;
- percentage of transfer missions with independent assessment and access evidence;
- user participation and evidence rejection rate.

Do not present synthetic or repository test results as customer outcomes. Establish the baseline in week 1 and export the final evidence in week 8.

## Commercial package

Use a fixed **€12,000 excluding VAT** pilot fee covering setup, facilitated discovery, weekly operating review and final executive report; 50% is due at kickoff and 50% at the final readout. Travel, bespoke integrations and third-party AI/storage overage are excluded. Treat this price as a sales hypothesis until at least five qualified buyer conversations and two paid pilots validate willingness to pay. Recurring pricing should scale with active employees or scoped operating units, with storage/AI overage and enterprise controls stated separately.

- [Sales/discovery/demo playbook](SALES_PLAYBOOK.md) and [pilot scorecard](PILOT_SCORECARD.md).

## Qualification and stop rules

Do not start a pilot without an executive sponsor, named validator, target process, measurable baseline and access to the people who hold the knowledge. Stop or rescope if the customer asks to use continuity scores for employee performance decisions; that is outside the product boundary.
