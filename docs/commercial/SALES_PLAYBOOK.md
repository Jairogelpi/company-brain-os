# Pilot sales playbook

## Qualification gate

Proceed only when all five are true:

1. a concrete continuity trigger exists;
2. an executive sponsor owns the risk;
3. one operational area can be scoped;
4. validators and knowledge holders will participate;
5. the buyer accepts the responsible-use boundary.

Disqualify employee-performance ranking, covert monitoring, “upload everything and let AI decide,” or a pilot with no baseline owner.

## Discovery questions

- What would stop or materially degrade if one named person were unavailable for 30 days?
- Which succession, audit, acquisition or retirement event creates urgency?
- How do you currently decide that a backup can perform the work?
- Which systems/access rights are required, and who can verify them?
- What evidence would the executive team accept as risk reduction?
- What data classes, retention, hosting and legal constraints apply?
- Who can approve the week-1 baseline and week-8 result?

## 30-minute demonstration

1. **Problem (3 min):** Pedro is the only expert for a critical filler setup.
2. **Capture (5 min):** submit an interview statement; show that AI only proposes.
3. **Truth (5 min):** approve claims and inspect provenance/explainable risk.
4. **False mitigation (4 min):** approve an SOP; show the person dependency remains.
5. **Transfer (8 min):** Laura demonstrates competency/access/evidence; independent reviewer approves.
6. **Outcome (3 min):** show closed mission, recalculated risk and audit trail.
7. **Next step (2 min):** select one customer area and baseline owner.

Use fictional data. Never demo real customer content without written authorization.

## Common objections

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| “We already have SharePoint/Notion.” | Keep it. Company Brain OS measures continuity controls and can reference existing artifacts; it does not compete on page editing. |
| “Employees will feel evaluated.” | The product scores dependencies and control evidence, not people. Agree the responsible-use rule before capture. |
| “AI can invent facts.” | AI cannot approve. Every canonical claim has a human approver and source/evidence trail. |
| “Documentation should be enough.” | An SOP reduces a documentation gap; execution risk remains until a backup proves capability and access. |
| “We cannot map the whole company.” | The pilot scopes one area and up to 25 exposures. Scale follows measured value. |

## Commercial experiment

- Default offer: eight weeks, €12,000 excluding VAT, scope defined in `ONE_PAGE.md`.
- Payment experiment: 50% kickoff / 50% final readout.
- After five qualified conversations, record win/loss reasons and price resistance.
- Do not set recurring pricing until at least two paid pilots provide usage, support and storage evidence.

Pipeline stages: qualified trigger → security/discovery → scoped baseline → signed pilot → weekly delivery → executive readout → scale/no-scale decision.
